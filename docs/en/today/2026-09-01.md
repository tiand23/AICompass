## Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1: Terminal-Bench-Science doubles, cache reads 75% cheaper

- **Summary**: Anthropic released its next-generation flagship models, Claude Fable 5.1 (available to everyone) and Claude Mythos 5.1 (limited to U.S. companies/individuals in Anthropic's trusted-access programs, with looser safeguards) — same base model, different safeguard levels. Benchmarks: Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 rose from Fable 5's 24.7% to 52.6% (more than doubling); on the Terminal-Bench 4.0 coding benchmark Fable 5.1 scores 55.8% (vs. 42.0% for Fable 5 and 52.3% for Opus 5), with Mythos 5.1 reaching 60.9% under its looser cyber safeguards; on the GDPval-AA v2 knowledge-work benchmark it scores 1,853 (vs. 1,824 for Opus 5). Cache-read cost drops 75%, cutting overall cost roughly 25% for typical workloads and up to 45% for highly agentic ones, while base pricing stays at $10/$50 per million tokens; cybersecurity false positives drop 60%. Both default to a 1M-token context window, 128k max output, always-on adaptive thinking; generated content carries a watermark and C2PA Content Credentials; both require 30-day data retention.
- **Why it matters**: This is Anthropic's next iteration at the top model tier after Opus 5, with large benchmark gains in both coding and scientific research (Terminal-Bench-Science more than doubling) while simultaneously pushing cost and safety false-positives down — "stronger, cheaper, fewer false blocks" advancing together, directly affecting selection and cost estimates for teams already building coding or research agents on the Fable/Mythos line (see [claude-models](/en/topics/claude-models)).

## Anthropic launches Enterprise Frontier Safeguards: zero data retention plus automated misuse monitoring with no human review

- **Summary**: Anthropic, working with 100+ enterprise customers (spanning financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, telecom, law, retail, and the public sector — including Goldman Sachs, Visa, Salesforce, and cloud providers AWS/Google Cloud/Azure) developed Enterprise Frontier Safeguards — a system combining a zero-data-retention privacy commitment with automated misuse detection. Enterprises can control how their data is reviewed, stored, and managed, and can enable fully automated safety monitoring that requires no human review at Anthropic. Rolling out in phases, aiming for broader availability this fall; offered at no charge, and the controls work whether access is direct or through a cloud provider. The change follows customer pushback on Anthropic's prior data-retention policy.
- **Why it matters**: This is an enterprise-grade answer to the "zero retention vs. misuse monitoring" tension, closely mirroring OpenAI's Private Safety Processing announced on 8/19 — both aim to monitor for abuse without inspecting raw content while preserving a ZDR commitment, and both emphasize requiring no human review. Two major vendors converging on similar answers to the same problem signals this has become an industry-level enterprise requirement, not one company's special case (see [agentic-safety](/en/topics/agentic-safety) ・ [enterprise-ai-agents](/en/topics/enterprise-ai-agents)).
