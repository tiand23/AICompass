## Barclays expands Claude use: three production use cases and a £2 billion savings target

- **Summary**: Anthropic publishes a Barclays case study: a target of 50% of the bank's developers using Claude Code by end of 2026 (rising further in 2027) for software development and legacy-system modernization; the Colleague Knowledge Assistant (RAG-based, launched 2025) has already processed over 1 million searches across 16,000+ UK staff users; the Global Markets team uses Claude to classify, enrich, and route roughly 120,000 incoming emails daily, cutting manual handling. This sits within a broader multi-year efficiency program targeting roughly £2 billion in savings.
- **Why it matters**: Joining the growing "agent production case library" alongside Toyota and Stripe, Barclays is a rare banking example disclosing quantified metrics across three distinct use cases at once (developer-adoption target, search volume/user coverage, daily email volume) tied directly to a company-wide financial target — a concrete reference point for peers sizing up the scale and pace of a Claude rollout.
