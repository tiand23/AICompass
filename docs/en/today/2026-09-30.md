## Claude Sonnet 4.5 announced for retirement, service ends 2026-11-30

- **Summary**: Anthropic formally announces the retirement timeline for `claude-sonnet-4-5-20250929`: service ends 2026-11-30, with users advised to migrate to Claude Sonnet 5.5, launched just two days earlier on 09-28.
- **Why it matters**: A two-month migration window is Anthropic's typical cadence for recent model retirements (compare Opus 4.1's retirement on 08-05) — integrations pinned to a fixed model ID need this date on their maintenance calendar. Combined with Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 both launching this month (09-22, 09-28), Claude's model-line generational turnover is concentrated within this single month.
