# 2026-10-12

## Azure publishes a deep-dive into its custom Maia 300 chip architecture, built for cloud-scale agentic inference

- **Summary**: Azure publishes a technical article detailing the architecture of its custom AI chip Maia 300, describing it as a unified platform purpose-built to meet the demands of "cloud-scale agentic inference."
- **Why it matters**: Following OpenAI/Broadcom's published benchmark results for the custom inference chip Jalapeño (08-25), another major cloud vendor is now explicitly positioning its custom inference silicon's design goal around "agentic inference" specifically — rather than general-purpose training or traditional inference. The competitive narrative around custom silicon is sharpening from "general-purpose compute" toward "serving the specific patterns of agent workloads" (long context, multi-step tool calls, high-concurrency sessions).
