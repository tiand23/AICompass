# 2026-08-02

## OpenAI Begins Rolling Out "Sign in with ChatGPT"

- **Summary**: OpenAI is rolling out "Sign in with ChatGPT" in beta across select plugins and partner sites — starting with Airtable, GitLab, HubSpot, Notion, Supabase and Vercel. Create or link a partner account with fewer steps using your ChatGPT account, then work with that service directly in ChatGPT or Codex; partners receive only name, email and profile picture, and each plugin's data access still requires separate review and approval.
- **Why it matters**: A step toward making ChatGPT an identity layer — the AI-era counterpart of "Sign in with Google". Two-sided for developers: integrating services get a low-friction acquisition and account-linking channel, while OpenAI gains another lock-in point upstream of agent workflows (the account system) — platform-dependency assessments now need to include the identity layer.
