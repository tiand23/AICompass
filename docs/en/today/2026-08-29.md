## OpenAI Ends Its Partnership With Cursor: Model Access Cut Off After SpaceX/xAI Acquires Cursor

- **Summary**: SpaceX agreed in June to acquire Cursor in stock (with an option to acquire it for $60B or pay a $10B break-up fee), and the deal closed on August 14. On August 29, OpenAI announced it was ending its partnership with Cursor, proposing November 12 as the date Cursor loses direct access to OpenAI's models — the maximum notice period allowed under the contract. OpenAI's stated reason: "We cannot be confident that SpaceX will use our technology within our terms of service, based on our experience with Elon Musk's companies violating contracts." Cursor CEO Michael Truell downplayed the impact, noting OpenAI models currently handle only about 5% of Cursor's traffic, with Anthropic stepping in to promise more Claude model support.
- **Why it matters**: A rare instance in the coding-agent ecosystem of a model vendor proactively cutting off a specific downstream customer's access — the direct cause being that the acquirer's (Musk-affiliated companies') existing trust and contract disputes with OpenAI have now extended into the model-access layer. For developers using Cursor, or any AI coding tool that could get acquired by or affiliated with a competing model vendor, this is a concrete supply-chain risk case: a tool's stability depends not just on its own technical approach, but on its ownership structure and its commercial relationship with upstream model vendors. The 5% traffic figure and Anthropic's quick backfill also suggest coding-agent tools have broadly built in multi-model redundancy already, so the real-world impact of a single-model cutoff may be limited (see [coding-agents](/en/topics/coding-agents)).

## three.ws Open-Sources: A Full-Stack Framework Giving AI Agents a Body, a Brain, a Wallet and a Job

- **Summary**: three.ws is an open-source platform (Apache 2.0) that unifies four previously separate capabilities into a single agent instance: ① **Body** — a 3D generation pipeline accepting text/photo/sketch input, routed across multiple generation engines (Microsoft TRELLIS, Hunyuan3D 2.1, TripoSR) chosen by latency and health with automatic failover, producing rigged, animatable glTF characters with textures; ② **Brain** — a multi-model agent runtime (not a single model) that routes requests across Claude, GPT-5.x, Grok and open models served via NVIDIA NIM, maintaining typed cross-session memory (user/feedback/project/reference), blending emotional state into expressions/gestures, and executing tools iteratively; ③ **Wallet** — an on-chain economic identity on Solana, deploying ERC-8004 contracts to twelve EVM networks via CREATE2, settling through x402 (the HTTP 402 payment protocol) so the agent can receive USDC micropayments per function call without human key custody — 110,416 on-chain USDC settlements had been completed as of August 25; ④ **Job** — multi-surface distribution: one-line web embeds, AR place-in-room links, 72 MCP servers in its official registry, ChatGPT plugins, Android via Trusted Web Activity, and terminal use via `npx` — letting a single agent instance exist simultaneously on the web, in chat clients, in physical space, and through voice interfaces. Fund-transfer actions pass through a 7-layer enforcement guard chain. It has official partnerships with OpenAI (Select Partner), NVIDIA (Inception member) and IBM (Business Partner).
- **Why it matters**: This is the first time this knowledge base has seen "3D embodiment, multi-model reasoning, on-chain economic identity, and multi-surface distribution" — four previously separately-evolving directions — unified into one open-source framework. The "wallet" piece in particular (the x402 protocol plus on-chain identity letting an agent autonomously send and receive micropayments without human involvement) fills in a thread on agent economic activity this knowledge base has rarely touched on; its official registry of 72 MCP servers is also a large-scale application sample of MCP at the distribution layer (see [mcp](/en/topics/mcp)). As a single community project its long-term influence remains to be seen, but the "embodiment + reasoning + payment + distribution" combination itself is a synthesis worth recording (see [agent-frameworks](/en/topics/agent-frameworks)).
