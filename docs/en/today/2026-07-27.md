## Anthropic States Its Position on Open-Weights Models

- **Summary**: Dario Amodei published a clarification that Anthropic has never advocated banning open-weights models, laying out two long-held concerns — authoritarian governments gaining military/control advantages through stronger models, and the impossibility of monitoring or recalling weights once released.
- **Why it matters**: The open-weights policy debate directly affects whether developers will keep getting strong open models; a frontier lab's public stance is a bellwether for that debate, and this post is also a direct response to accusations of restricting open source under the guise of safety.
