# 2026-09-18

## Anthropic and Accenture partner to build a team of "embedded evaluators"

- **Summary**: Anthropic and Accenture announce a partnership in which Accenture's AI-focused business Faculty leads a team of "embedded evaluators" with access comparable to an employee's, working inside Anthropic on model red-teaming, alignment assessments, and guardrail testing — able to watch models take shape during training, follow decisions on how models get built and deployed, and talk directly to staff; both companies plan to invest at least $1 billion each over the next five years in AI safety work, with Anthropic funding Accenture's side directly; the partnership is non-exclusive, and Anthropic says it's also in discussions with third parties like METR.
- **Why it matters**: This institutionalizes the "evaluation transparency as the new baseline for frontier labs" thread (tracked since the 07-30 Frontier Red Team retrospective) — upgrading third-party evaluation from after-the-fact retrospective reports to a resident evaluation team with internal access, offering a concrete, replicable organizational model for how a frontier lab can demonstrate it deserves trust.
