# World Models

## Overview

World models / generative simulation: learning environment dynamics to generate realistic simulated environments in real time, for robot training, validation and synthetic data production. Representative line: NVIDIA Cosmos.

## Why It Matters

Generative simulation is becoming infrastructure for embodied AI and robotics — especially in domains where real data is expensive and dangerous (e.g. healthcare). Vertical production deployments (like surgical robotics) signal the shift from demos to production.

## Core Concepts

- **Generative simulation**: generative models producing environment feedback in real time, replacing hand-built simulators.
- **Synthetic training data**: simulation output used to train downstream policy models.
